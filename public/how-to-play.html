How to Play

Choose a match format and select Play Match. Drive into the ball to score in the opposing goal. Collect boost pads, defend your goal and use jumps to reach airborne balls.

Desktop controls

WASD or arrow keys: drive and steer. Shift: boost. Space: jump or dodge. E: drift. C: ball camera. Escape or P: pause. M: mute. R: reset kickoff.

Mobile controls

Rotate to landscape for the best experience. Use the left and right steering buttons and the acceleration, brake, jump and boost buttons. You can enable touch controls in Settings. Open Customize Controls from Settings to drag the driving buttons, Ball Cam and speed/nitro gauge, adjust size and opacity, choose a left-handed preset, and save your layout in the current browser. Reset restores the default layout; Cancel discards unsaved changes.

Performance tips

Choose Low graphics if gameplay is slow. Close other demanding tabs and use a browser with WebGL support.
