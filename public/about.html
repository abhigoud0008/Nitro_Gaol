About Nitro Goal Arena

Nitro Goal Arena is an independent browser-based 3D vehicle football game. Drive a sports car, challenge computer-controlled opponents and score goals in a stadium.

Game features

Choose 1v1 or 2v2 matches, customize cars in the garage, select day, sunset or night lighting, and adjust graphics for your device. The game uses Three.js and WebGL. Online multiplayer is not currently offered.

This independent project is not affiliated with Rocket League or its publishers.
